# Landing Page Plan — Care Study Academic Support

> Goal: a landing page that reads like a **professional nursing academic support agency** whose hallmark discipline is the Patient/Family Care Study — *not* "an AI tool that writes care studies."
>
> Audience: **final-year student nurses in Ghana** (diploma & degree, NMTCs and universities) who must complete and **defend** a Patient/Family Care Study to qualify for licensure through the **Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC)**.

---

## 1. The market, in one page

### 1.1 The Ghanaian context (the opportunity)

- The **Patient/Family Care Study is a graduation and licensure requirement** for final-year nursing & midwifery students in Ghana. Studies are submitted through colleges and, per college repositories, serve "as a requirement for the award of a professional license to practice by the Nurses and Midwives Council of Ghana."
- Students must complete the full document **and defend it in a viva** before supervisors/panels.
- The NMC has been working on **digitizing the Patient/Family Care Study** since 2021 — the whole system is moving toward structured, digital submission. A professional digital drafting standard fits the direction the Council itself is heading.
- Yet almost no structured support exists: supervision time is scarce, the format is passed down informally, and the reference material (textbooks, formularies) is expensive.

### 1.2 What students actually do today (the current "help")

| Channel | What it offers | The problem |
|---|---|---|
| **Ghostwriters** (Instagram/WhatsApp agencies, "$6–25/page" mills) | Write the whole care study for you; some offer "viva preparation" | Expensive, ethically indefensible, and *fails at the defense* — a student who didn't write it cannot answer one question about it. Risk of being reported to the college. |
| **Shared samples** (Studocu, CourseHero, past students' studies) | Pre-written care studies to copy/adapt | Plagiarism risk; content doesn't match *your* patient; out-of-date citations. |
| **Generic AI (ChatGPT etc.)** | Free drafting | Doesn't know the Ghanaian format, fabricates patient facts and citations, no grounding in your notes or your school's template, no defense readiness. |
| **YouTube/TikTok guides** | Advice videos | Advice only — no output, no document. |

### 1.3 International software competitors (the closest analogues)

| Product | What it is | Positioning | Gap vs. us |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CarePlanHQ** (careplanhq.com, $4.99/mo student) | AI care plan generator — NANDA-I diagnoses, NIC interventions, NOC outcomes, PDF export | "Evidence-based care plans in minutes, not hours" — time-saving, clinical rigor, US accreditation standards | Produces a **care plan** (one chapter of the study), US NANDA/NIC/NOC format, from a fixed assessment form. Cannot produce a Ghanaian 5–6 chapter care study, cannot use *your* patient notes in depth, no citations from your sources, no viva prep. |
| **Care Plan Genie** (NURSING.com) | Care plan generator inside a study/NCLEX platform | Same NANDA care-plan niche, bundled with exam prep | Same as above. |
| **Heidi Health** | AI scribe for *practising* nurses | Clinical documentation, not education | Wrong audience. |
| **MeducationAI / GoodNurse etc.** | AI study tools & NCLEX prep | Exam prep, study guides, care plan help as content | They teach *about* care studies; none produce the document. |
| **SimpleNursing, Picmonic, NRSNG** | Study apps / NCLEX prep | Learning content, not document production | Not a drafting service at all. |

**The international software space is US-centric and care-*plan*-shaped.** Nobody international builds the full Patient/Family Care Study document to an African nursing council's format, grounded in the student's own ward notes and their own reference library.

### 1.4 How we stand out (the honest differentiators)

1. **The whole document, not a chapter.** We support the complete care study — from front matter and patient particulars through history, examination, investigations, the nursing care plan, implementation, evaluation, and bibliography — in the Ghanaian 5–6 chapter structure.
2. **It stays *your* study.** Every draft is built from the student's own clinical notes. No invented patient facts, no fabricated data — which makes the study genuinely defensible in the viva (and ethically clean).
3. **Grounded in your sources, cited properly.** The engine drafts against the student's own uploaded textbooks, WHO fact sheets, formularies, and past care studies — producing real in-text citations and a reference list. No fake citations.
4. **School-conformant formatting.** Academic Word export (chapter structure, headings, tables, Times New Roman, line spacing) that matches what colleges expect to receive.
5. **Reference verification.** Every citation is checked against the sources used, and gaps are flagged — a quality bar ghostwriters and ChatGPT can't offer.
6. **The Viva Preparation Programme.** We do not hand over a document and disappear. Every completed study is paired with defense preparation — mock viva, a question bank built from the student's own study, and guided drills — so the student can genuinely answer for their work.
7. **The support position.** We are an academic support agency: the study is built from the student's *own* clinical materials and school guidelines — never a generic template, never invented patient facts — and we stay with the student from order to defense.

## 2. Positioning

### 2.1 The one-line position

> **We are a nursing academic support agency. Our hallmark is the Patient/Family Care Study — we prepare a complete, well-cited, professionally formatted study from your clinical materials, and we prepare you to defend it with confidence in the viva.**

### 2.2 What we say / what we never say

**Say (professional, agency voice):**
- "Nursing academic support" / "academic support for nursing education"
- "The Care Study Support Programme" — the flagship service, in two parts: **Study Preparation** and **Viva Preparation**
- "Prepared by our academic team from your own clinical materials"
- "Evidence-based, properly cited, school-conformant"
- "We prepare the study. We prepare you to defend it."

**Never say on the page:**
- ❌ "AI generates your care study" / "care study generator"
- ❌ Essay-mill phrasing: "do my assignment", "cheap care study", "pay for a passed project"
- ❌ "ChatGPT-powered", "AI tool", "prompt"
- ❌ Anything that reads like an essay mill or a shortcut

The technology exists and is real — it's just *behind* the method, presented as "our drafting technology" or "our academic production team," never as the headline. Transparency lives in the FAQ ("how is my study prepared?"), framed as: *our academic team, supported by drafting technology, prepares your study from the materials you provide — nothing is ever invented, and your materials stay yours.*

### 2.3 The story (narrative arc)

Every professional-services page is a story. Ours:

- **The hero:** the final-year Ghanaian student nurse — capable, clinically experienced, doing real ward work.
- **The villain:** the Care Study — a document that is simultaneously (a) a licensure requirement of the Nursing and Midwifery Council, (b) a defended viva, (c) a 60+ page academic document in a format no one formally teaches, and (d) a task that lands on students who are already stretched across wards, study, and life.
- **The bad guides:** ghostwriters who write it *for* you (and leave you defenceless), shared samples (plagiarism), generic chatbots (fabrication).
- **The guide (us):** a professional academic support agency that treats the Care Study as a discipline — with a method, standards, and a support programme.
- **The method (our promise):** *You bring your patient and your materials. We bring the academic method.* You place your order with your clinical data and guidelines; our academic team produces a complete, cited, formatted study; then the Viva Preparation Programme ensures you can defend every line of it.
- **The stakes:** licensure, registration, the start of a professional career.
- **The ending:** "She walked out of her defense having answered every question — because we prepared the study *and* the student."

### 2.4 Brand name proposals

The current name ("CareStudy Drafting Assistant") is a tool name. An agency needs an institutional name. Options, in order of my preference:

1. **CareStudy Institute** — institutional, calm, professional; "institute" implies standards and method.
2. **The Care Study Bureau** — old-world professional-services feel; "bureau" = a practice that does one thing seriously.
3. **Nightingale Academic Services** — the Florence Nightingale hook is strong: *Notes on Nursing* was the first disciplined account of bedside care. Tagline-ready: "From bedside notes to defended study." (Risk: common name, check availability.)
4. **CareStudy Ghana** — clear local anchor, but limits international expansion later.
5. **The Nursing Scholarship Centre** — broader (essays, projects, theses later), care study as flagship.

Whatever the name, keep the programme name: **"The Care Study Support Programme"** — with the two named services, **Study Preparation** and **Viva Preparation**. The internal production software is the "studio"; students only ever see their dashboard.

### 2.5 Tone & visual direction

- **Tone:** institutional, scholarly, quietly confident. Think university faculty page or professional body — not a SaaS startup, not a WhatsApp agency.
- **Visual:** deep clinical navy/teal, ivory, restrained gold accents; serif display type (the app already uses serif in its brand tile — carry that up); photography of real Ghanaian clinical settings and student nurses; no cartoonish "AI" imagery.
- **Typography/copy rhythm:** short, declarative sentences; frequent pull-quotes; no hype words ("revolutionary", "supercharge").

## 3. Page structure (section by section, with copy angles)

### 0. Navigation
Institutional nav: **About · The Programme · Method · Standards · FAQ · Contact** + CTA **"Start your care study"**.

- **Eyebrow:** "Nursing Academic Support Agency — Accra · Nationwide"

Our hallmark is the Care Study Support Programme — a complete, well-cited Patient/Family Care Study prepared from your clinical materials, and the preparation you need to defend it."*
- **CTAs:** "Start your care study" (primary) · "See how it works" (secondary)
- **Trust strip (below hero):** "Aligned with the Nursing and Midwifery Council's move to digital care studies" · "Grounded in your own clinical data" · "Every citation traceable to a real source"

### 2. The stakes (problem section)
- Headline: *"A requirement of your profession. A defence before your college. A document no one ever taught you to write."*
- Three cards (the villain made concrete):
  1. **Required.** The Patient/Family Care Study is a graduation and licensure requirement of the Nursing and Midwifery Council.
  2. **Defended.** You will stand before your supervisors and defend it — every diagnosis, every intervention, every reference.
  3. **Undersupported.** Supervision time is scarce, the format is passed down informally, and reference texts are expensive.
- Close with the wrong answers already out there (one line each): ghostwriters who leave you defenceless; copied samples that risk your registration; chatbots that invent facts and citations. → *"There is a better way."*

### 3. Who we are (the agency)
- Headline: *"An academic support agency for nursing education."*
- Body: we are a team of nursing educators, clinicians, and academic specialists. Our work is the quiet infrastructure of nursing education — standards, structure, and scholarly guidance. The Care Study is our hallmark discipline because it is where students most need a professional partner and least have one.
- A short "our standards" band: **Accuracy · Integrity · Built on your materials · Defense readiness** (each with one sentence).

### 4. How the programme works (the heart of the page)
The client journey, 5 steps. The student interacts only with their own dashboard; the production happens in our studio behind the scenes.
1. **Create your account.** Every student gets a private dashboard — your own workspace for your project, from order to delivery.
2. **Place your order.** You send us your project information as an order: your clinical notes and patient data, your college's care-study guidelines, and any reference documents you want used. The order arrives in our production studio.
3. **Our academic team produces your study.** Your materials become a complete, cited, school-conformant study. You watch the status from your dashboard — *submitted → in production → ready*.
4. **Receive your finished study.** Download your complete study as a professionally formatted academic document, with real citations and a reference list.
5. **Prepare for the viva.** The Viva Preparation Programme: a mock defense, a question bank built from *your* study, and guided drills — so you walk in able to answer for every diagnosis, intervention, and reference.

*Supporting detail blocks:* chapter-by-chapter coverage; citation & reference-list engine; reference verification & gap flagging; academic Word export; the client dashboard (ordering, uploads, status tracking, delivery); the Viva Preparation Programme.

### 5. What you get (the document)
- Mockup/screenshot of the **student dashboard**: placing an order, uploading materials, status tracking, downloading the finished study — plus a shot of the finished document (chapter list, care-plan table, Word export).
- Feature list, stated as outcomes:
  - Your own student dashboard — place orders, upload materials, track progress, download finished work
  - A complete care study — front matter to bibliography
  - Real in-text citations and a generated reference list
  - Verified references (gaps and inconsistencies flagged, not hidden)
  - Word document in professional academic formatting, ready for submission
  - The Viva Preparation Programme — mock defense, question bank, and drills

### 6. Built on your materials, defended by you (the quality section)
- Headline: *"Prepared from your materials. Defended by you, with confidence."*
- Body: your study is built from *your* clinical data and *your* college's guidelines — never a generic template, never an invented patient. And because you must still stand before your supervisors, every study is paired with the Viva Preparation Programme, so you know your study well enough to answer for it. A solid study and a prepared student — that is the difference between professional support and a shortcut.
- (This section is the anti-"hand you a document and vanish" argument: other services deliver and disappear; we stay through the defense.)

### 7. Social proof
- Testimonials (to be collected): final-year students who passed their defense; a nursing tutor who supervised multiple studies.
- Optional: "Trusted by students at [named colleges] — with permission" (only if real).
- Metrics only if real: "X studies guided to defense", "X chapters drafted" — never fabricated.

### 8. For institutions (short, optional section)
- One paragraph + CTA: *"Nursing colleges: provide your students with structured care-study support aligned with your guidelines. Talk to us about institutional access."* (A future revenue line and a credibility signal even if not sold today.)

### 9. How to start / pricing (account-based ordering)
- **Create your account → place your order → track it in your dashboard → receive your study.** The order flow *is* the pricing story: students order per project, paying when they place the order with their materials.
- Optional add-on, shown as part of the programme (not hidden in fine print): the **Viva Preparation Programme** — bundled with the study or per-session, TBD.
- CTA: "Create your account & place your order" · "Book a free consultation".

### 10. FAQ (handles the honest questions)
- "How is my study prepared?" → *Our academic team, supported by drafting technology, prepares your study from the materials you provide — your clinical notes, your patient data, and your college's guidelines. Nothing is ever invented, and your materials stay yours.* (Honest, and the only acceptable answer given the positioning.)
- "Do I need to see your production studio?" → *No. Your dashboard is the only interface you need: place your order, upload your materials, watch the status, download your finished study.*
- "What is the Viva Preparation Programme?" → *A mock defense with your study as the script, a question bank built from the chapters and care plan, and guided drills on the questions panels most often ask — so the study is yours to defend, not just yours to submit.*
- "Is this allowed by my college?" → "We recommend checking your college's specific policy, as with any academic support service. What we guarantee is a study built from your own materials — and a student prepared to defend it."
- "Will my supervisors tell?" → "The study is prepared from your data and you are trained to answer for it — that is the point of the programme."
- "Which chapters does the programme cover?" → all of them, listed.
- "Can it match my school's format?" → yes — upload your guideline/template and the structure adapts.
- "What about data privacy?" → your patient data is anonymized per professional standards; your study is yours (stored privately, never shared).

### 11. Footer
Name, "Nursing Academic Support Agency", contact, links to the programme, integrity policy, and a note: *"CareStudy Institute supports nursing education — preparing the study, and preparing you to defend it."*
