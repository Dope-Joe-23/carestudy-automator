Reference library sources
========================================
The bundled library contains only authoritative, openly licensed medical
references — WHO fact sheets and the WHO Model Formulary, MedlinePlus health
topics (public domain), and the Open RN nursing textbook. Wikipedia articles
were removed in August 2026 in favour of these sources.
